# Fern Hollow — browser playtest

Play at **https://www.edianibarrola.com/dinogame/**.

This repository contains the published Unity WebGL player. Editable Unity and Blender source belongs in the private `dinogamesource` repository.

Choose Rex, Raptor, Triceratops or Ankylosaurus. Resume your saved life, hatch or try an adult. Each species retains its own local bloodline and eggs. Growth takes 12 healthy active minutes; Test tools offer stage selection and 5x growth/incubation. Healthy adults can meet a same-species mate, lay two eggs and hatch a ready offspring. Functional inheritance and cosmetics remain deferred.

PC: WASD/arrows, Shift sprint, Ctrl sneak, hold Space/mouse for a forward bite, Q/right click for Roar/Pounce/Charge/rear Tail sweep, hold E to eat, hold F to drink. Tab/Esc opens bloodlines, T opens Test tools, N lays an eligible nest. Phone: landscape, floating joystick anywhere outside the action buttons; toggle Sprint/Sneak, hold Bite/Eat/Drink and tap the separate species ability button.

Explore a bounded island with beach, plains, forest and three freshwater pools. Ocean water is undrinkable. Small crabs are hatchling prey; carcasses provide different finite food amounts. Raptors are threats; herbivores defend when provoked. Crabs and mammals protect territory against hatchlings and flee grown dinosaurs. Connected tree crowns, reliable mate lookup and persistent AI resource routes improve the island playtest. Orbiting gold stars show stuns, and small icons replace AI behavior labels. Compact survival meters and action docks keep the play view clearer; Test tools are accessed through bloodlines or T. This is an early single-player playtest. Saves belong to this browser/device; real phone performance, caves and multiplayer remain future work.

Serve `index.html` over HTTP/HTTPS. Unity gzip fallback files use `.unityweb`, allowing GitHub Pages hosting without custom compression headers. `.nojekyll` keeps player files intact. No private source or credentials are included.
